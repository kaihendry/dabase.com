I recommend [Feather Hi-Stainless double-edge razor blades](https://www.feather.co.jp/en).
They're made in Japan, and mine are the ones in the yellow pack labelled **New Hi-Stainless**:
ten platinum-coated blades in a little white dispenser.

![Feather New Hi-Stainless dispenser with its yellow label and ten-blade count](feather-yellow-pack.avif)

My Amazon invoice from **4 June 2022** records **£15.99 for 50 blades**, including VAT, with
free delivery. That works out at **about 32p per blade**, at 2022 prices.
I get about **five shaves from one blade**.

The appeal is simple: keep the razor and replace the blade. Once you have the handle,
the blades are a cheap way to keep shaving.

My razor is a German [MÜHLE](https://www.muehle-shaving.com/en/), pictured here with the
individually wrapped Feather blades:

![My metal MÜHLE safety razor beside an open dispenser of wrapped Feather blades](muhle-razor-and-feather-blades.avif)

If your shave isn't close enough, reapply the shaving foam and go again. Closest shave
you'll ever have.

One useful detail on the dispenser: there's a slot on the back marked **For used blades**.
The same little box has somewhere to put the old blades as you work through the new ones.

![Back of the Feather dispenser showing the slot marked For used blades](used-blade-slot.avif)
