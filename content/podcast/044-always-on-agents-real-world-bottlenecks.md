AI agents can keep working while we are away from the keyboard. What does that mean for our time, our independence and the systems that still have to ship the code?

In episode 44 of AI Infrastructure, Kai Hendry and Vincent De Smet compare Orca, Claude remote control and Hermes reviews, and take a look at stacked pull requests. We debate Omarchy and open-source politics, the cost of frontier models versus local inference, and why agents still need a responsible human.

We also discuss helping the next generation learn engineering through product ownership, whether specialist agent roles help, and why CI keeps becoming a bottleneck. Along the way: Backstage and MCP, Spotify’s agent adoption, and collaborative feedback on AI artifacts.
