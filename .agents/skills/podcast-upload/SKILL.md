# AI Infrastructure podcast uploads

Use the approved episode assets and [podcast publishing checklist](../../../docs/podcast-publishing.md#7-user-uploads-the-full-video-to-youtube). Follow the user's requested upload or handoff workflow.

## Earnings / Monetization

Set **Earn / Monetization → Watch Page ads & YouTube Premium → On** for full podcast episodes, including **Unlisted** uploads, unless the user explicitly requests otherwise. Continue with **Next** or **Done**, then save the setting.

This is Kai's standing preference. Do not copy **Off** from an older episode or stale upload notes. Include **Monetization: On** in any episode-specific upload instructions or handoff you write.

After saving, reopen the video's **Earn / Monetization** page and verify **On** persisted before reporting completion. Keep the requested visibility and verify **AI Infrastructure** playlist membership separately, as described in the publishing checklist.
