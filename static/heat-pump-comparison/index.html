What differs in everyday use

Hot water

Our tank holds 200 litres; his holds 210 litres — only a small difference. Ours is set to 50°C and heats on a cheap overnight schedule, with extra top-ups during cheap daytime periods. His app was set to maintain 55°C. Both have a backup electric water heater.

Controls and monitoring

He mainly uses the Vaillant phone app. We also use Home Assistant, software that tracks the system and automates hot-water top-ups. Our extra monitoring helps us spot problems; it does not by itself prove that our heat pump is more efficient.

What winter will tell us

Neither account yet shows a full winter of use. Early efficiency numbers are encouraging, but cover different conditions. The useful test is whether every room stays comfortable, there is enough hot water, and the electricity cost is reasonable.

Two things to check with Reco

  • Radiators: ask for the room-by-room calculations showing why each replacement was needed.
  • Outdoor sensor: after a power cut, ours lost contact and the system heated the house unnecessarily. If this remains unresolved, ask Reco to check its signal and position.
